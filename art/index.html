See the art while you play FlatMMO

Get the FlatMMO Art Panel: festive versions of NPCs and buildings drawn by players, plus a 🎨 button to browse approved art and (if you dare) turn on a 🎃 jumpscare.

  1. Install the Tampermonkey browser extension.
  2. Click Install the art panel and press Install in the tab that opens.
  3. Open FlatMMO and click the 🎨 button in the bottom-left corner.
Logins are shared with Idle MMO, so if you have a game account, use that.
