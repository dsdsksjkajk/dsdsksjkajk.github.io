    Owner tools: backfill trade data

    Recent trades are pulled in automatically on a schedule, so you shouldn't normally need this. It's here for backfilling history the automatic scraper can't reach — older trades from Discord, say. Paste trades below, then download the updated file and upload it to replace trades.json in your hosting — the automatic updates only ever add newly scraped trades on top of whatever's already in the file, so your backfilled rows stay put. Anyone can open this panel, but it only ever produces a file for you to download — nothing is sent anywhere, so it's safe to leave visible.

    Bargain finder

    Paste the market's current listings below and this checks each one against its usual trade price, so items listed unusually cheap stand out. Paste in the same style as the trade importer — item name, then quantity and price. Nothing typed here is saved: it's a one-off calculation that clears when you leave or reload the page.

    Fan-made and not affiliated with FlatMMO. Prices come from the public Recent trades list on market.flatmmo.com, which gives dates but not times and only covers a recent window, so the site owner collects it by hand and republishes it periodically — see "Data last published" above for how current it is. The bargain finder never sends or saves anything you paste into it; it's recalculated locally in your browser and gone when you leave the page.
